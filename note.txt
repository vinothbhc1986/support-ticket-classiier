What is PII?

    PII stands for Personally Identifiable Information — any data that could be used to identify, contact, or locate a specific individual.
    Eg: payment cards

PII Redaction:

    PII Redaction is the process of identifying and masking (or removing) sensitive personal details from text before it is processed, stored, or sent to an external service.
